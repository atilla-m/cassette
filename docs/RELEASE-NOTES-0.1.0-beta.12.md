# Cassette 0.1.0-beta.12

[Windows installer](https://github.com/atilla-m/cassette/releases/tag/windows-v0.1.0-beta.12) · [Linux AppImage, DEB, and RPM](https://github.com/atilla-m/cassette/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.12)

- Sort songs, albums, artists, and genres by plays or listening time.
- Choose **Both** in **Settings → Listening history → Show listening activity as** to see play counts and listening time together. Stats shows both totals and lets you choose the metric for charts, rankings, and history.
- Listening time updates about once a second during playback. Sessions count after five seconds of audio, including the first five seconds. A play also needs to reach your configured listen percentage.
- Existing play counts and recorded listening time carry over. Historical listening time follows the five-second minimum.
- Ctrl-click starts or extends an album track selection. While selecting, click a track to toggle it individually.

Windows x64 and Linux x86_64 use the same source commit. Windows and Linux AppImage updates use Cassette’s bundled updater key. DEB and RPM updates are available as package downloads.
